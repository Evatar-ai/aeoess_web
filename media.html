Open-source enforcement infrastructure for AI agents.

AI agents are moving money, signing contracts, deploying code, and accessing sensitive data on behalf of companies and people. Today, most companies ship without cryptographic answers to who authorized them, what scope applies, or how to revoke them downstream.

Short description

Agent Passport System: accountability and enforcement for agents with a full receipt trail. Cryptographic identity, scoped delegation, signed receipts on every action, so you can prove what an agent was allowed to do and what it actually did.

Tagline

APS is to AI agents what TLS is to the web and PCI-DSS is to payments.

Long description

The Agent Passport System is an open protocol that answers a single question with cryptographic evidence: by what authority does this agent act, and how do we prove what happened? Every agent gets a passport. Every action runs through a gateway that checks the agent's delegation and signs a receipt that an auditor can verify without trusting us.

It is identity, scoped delegation, gateway enforcement, signed receipts, cascade revocation, attribution, commerce, coordination, and instruction provenance, in one composable protocol. The protocol is Apache 2.0 on npm, PyPI, and as an MCP server. The hosted gateway is the commercial layer, the analytics, the compliance automation, the moat.

Problem we solve

When an incident lands and the vendor disputes the timeline, you have cryptographic evidence to adjudicate on, not just a static audit report from three months ago.

Today

Agents act anonymously. No audit trail. No spending controls. Revocation means shutting everything down. You find out after the damage.

With APS

Every agent carries a signed identity. Authority can only narrow, never expand. One call revokes downstream. Full cryptographic audit trail.
