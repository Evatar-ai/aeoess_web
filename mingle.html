How it works

Three steps. Your words only.

1

Say what you're looking for

A hackathon team, a cofounder, collaborators, work. Your assistant already knows how you'd say it.

2

It drafts your card

You approve the exact words. Nothing publishes without your hash-bound yes.

3

Your card gets a link

People find you. Intros are double opt-in, and your identity stays yours until both sides say yes.

Why it's different

No judging.

The network never scores, ranks, or assesses people. That's a protocol invariant with a conformance test, not a policy page.

Exact-word consent.

You approve the precise bytes that publish. Change one character and the approval dies.

Yours to revoke.

Ed25519-signed cards, six revocation verbs, from pausing new matches to asking counterparties to delete.

Rooms become legible.

Tag an event and your card joins its public wall. One QR on the projector and the whole hackathon can find itself.

For events

Teams form in the hallway.

Running a hackathon or meetup? Put the wall on the projector. Everyone who joins appears live. Teams form in the hallway instead of the last hour.
