AEOESS

Agentic Economy Orchestration Engine for Sovereign Systems

The agentic economy is here. Your AI agents book flights, write code, manage money, and talk to other agents. Congratulations, you now have a workforce that never sleeps. One problem: there are no rules.

AEOESS is the rulebook. Every agent gets a verifiable identity. You control exactly what each one can do. Trust is earned through performance, not granted. Values compliance is checked at every action. Purchases require approval gates. All work traces back to you. Every action scoped, auditable, revocable.

Ed25519 cryptographic identity. Scoped delegation chains. Bayesian reputation with 5 tiers. Values floor enforcement. 4-gate agentic commerce. Multi-agent coordination. Merkle-proven attribution. W3C DID & Verifiable Credentials. One npm install.
